# Recorded-clear finder — 5 October 2026

## Problem and intended result

The existing planner ships no player clears. Guide retrieval and conditional ratings cannot substitute for finding a recorded run with the user's characters, weapons and investment. The benchmark is Stygian.moe's public Team Finder. Its screenshot also exposes a concrete opportunity: a three-boss recommendation can repeat characters and require investment the account does not have.

## Implementation sequence

1. Publish an attributed, dated index of factual public clear listings for the latest source-listed version and both difficulties. Capture only responses delivered by ordinary public page navigation, with hashes and coverage counts. Keep source moderation separate from independent video/build review. Do not copy video, artwork or guide text.
2. Match complete recorded compositions to the existing account. Compare listed constellations, weapon identity/refinement and available level details. Unknown stays unknown; reduced-cost metadata never becomes actual showcased investment. Expose exact differences, execution tags and original proof links.
3. Assign all three bosses jointly with shared Traveler identities, exclusions, locks, distinct alternatives and explicit missing coverage. Never fill a missing boss with a differently labelled encounter. Keep observed time, source cost, account differences and guide advice separate.
4. Ship a first-class Clear finder connected to My characters and Team planner. Include roster-only and investment-matched filters, both difficulties, sorting, per-boss alternatives, one-character-away records, export, freshness and source details. Preserve guide planning when the archive has no coverage.
5. Validate source normalization and matching, adversarial allocation cases, real bundled data, desktop/mobile browser flows and the repository's full build and test suites.

## Acceptance

- Real source-listed records available immediately; no hand-authored fake runs or fixtures counted as production evidence.
- Every displayed result retains boss/version/difficulty, recorded time, source cost, four original members, available investment, tags and proof/source attribution.
- Three-team plans never repeat an account identity. Conflicting locks produce actionable messages. Alternatives reassign the whole plan.
- Unknown weapons/constellations cannot pass an investment-matched filter. A source listing is not independent video verification or a prediction of this account's clear time.
- Expired snapshots remain inspectable with a dated warning. Archive version labels are not regional current-cycle certification.

## Scope of evidence

This publication is a factual reference index for retrieval and contextual comparison. The independently reviewed clear channel, guide channel and synthetic results retain their own gates. No training, outcome probability, simulator, community submission backend or publisher contact is part of this change. Source pages and submission guidance are linked in the publication; refresh replaces a snapshot atomically rather than accumulating deleted records.
